Last updated: November 17, 2026

Clip-Watcher is a personal desktop tool that creates short clips from Twitch live streams and uploads them as drafts to the user's own TikTok account via the TikTok Content Posting API. Nothing is published automatically; the user reviews and posts every draft in the TikTok app.

By using Clip-Watcher you agree to:

  • use it only with TikTok accounts you own or are authorized to manage,
  • only upload content you have the right to share, and credit the original creators,
