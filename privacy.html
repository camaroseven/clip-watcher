Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Clip-Watcher runs entirely on the user's own computer. The developer does not operate any servers and does not collect, store, sell or share personal data.

Data accessed via TikTok (only after the user logs in and consents):

  • basic profile information (display name, open ID) to show which account is connected,
  • permission to upload videos as drafts to the user's TikTok inbox (video.upload).

Access tokens are stored only locally on the user's computer and are used solely to communicate with TikTok's official API. Videos are sent only to TikTok.

Deleting your data: delete the local file tiktok.json and/or remove Clip-Watcher's access in the TikTok app under Settings > Security > Apps and services.
